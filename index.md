Hi! I'm [Nana Adjei Manu](https://github.com/naamanu).

I'm a Product Engineer at [charles](https://hello-charles.com) in Berlin, an
AI-native conversational marketing and commerce platform, where I build
customer-facing product across the messaging and commerce experience.

Product engineering, for me, means owning the whole slice, from database schema
to pixels, from idea to production, and the metrics after launch. I've done that
for eight years across frontend, backend, and infrastructure at
[SuitePad](https://www.suitepad.de),
[HousingAnywhere](https://housinganywhere.com),
[Andela](https://andela.com), [Rhino](https://rhino.com), and
[Leafly](https://leafly.com).

Alongside that, I keep up an independent research and writing practice at the
intersection of language theory and practical systems. I hold an MSc in Advanced
Computer Science from the [University of Leicester](https://le.ac.uk), where my
thesis focused on operational semantics. I care about type systems, fast
interfaces, and software that doesn't fall over.

Most of my writing lives on the [blog](/blog/): operational semantics, type
systems, functional programming, and the occasional detour into how I think
about learning and the craft of building things. If you're new here,
[how I became an engineer](/blog/how-i-became-an-engineer/) is a good place to
start, or browse everything by [topic](/tags/).
